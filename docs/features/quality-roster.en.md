# Quality Roster

The **Quality Roster** keeps a journal of quality values observed on objects and resources.

![Quality Roster](../images/quality%20raster.png)

## Two Q buttons in Xtensions menu

- **Q:** Open the Quality Roster and browse recorded entries, including buildings, trees, seeds, saplings, and barrels.
- **Q with an arrow:** Activate the quality-recording cursor. Select an object to read its quality and record it in the journal.

## Planting material

The **Farming** tab includes crop seeds and planting material such as carrots,
turnips, onions and whole garlic bulbs. Choose **Farming → Add**, then left-click a bulb
or stack in an inventory. The journal records the best quality among the stack's
members and the number of members at that quality; red and yellow onions have
separate records.

![Warning when the planting reserve is better than the recorded quality](../images/better%20quality.png)

Field processing records planting stock automatically when sowing starts. A new
or improved record requires at least five usable items of the same quality,
counted across the inspected storage and backpack. This includes roots and bulbs.
Higher or equal historical records are retained; different qualities are not
combined to meet the threshold. All crop profiles supported by field processing
are also accepted by the journal. Manual recording remains available for smaller
quantities. Existing records are retained after the tab rename.

## Quality on plans

Recorded quality values can also appear on the **Village Plan** or **Personal Claim Plan**, allowing you to identify useful trees and production equipment in context. A plan cannot show quality that has not been recorded.
